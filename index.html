The SRA Difference

Education Built Around the Child, Not the Test.

We do not associate academic rigor with test-taking anxiety, hours of passive homework, or rote memorization. Rigor is critical thinking, joyful engagement, and mastery through hands-on exploration.

Hands-On, Multi-Sensory Rigor

From dissecting specimens and building robotics to Singapore Math problem-solving, students touch, experiment, and question. Learning sticks because it is deeply felt and understood.

Learn our philosophy →

Structured Literacy & Science of Reading

Our language arts instruction is grounded in proven cognitive neuroscience. Every student masters decoding, phonemic awareness, vocabulary, and written expression with systematic precision.

Explore curriculum →

Nature Classrooms & Joyful Play

Research confirms that physical movement, outdoor investigation, and laughter accelerate brain development. Children at SRA spend meaningful time outdoors discovering ecology and leadership.

See outdoor learning →

Dedicated Dyslexia Center

Certified Orton-Gillingham practitioners provide 1-on-1 tutoring integrated directly into the student’s daily schedule—eliminating after-school commute stress for families.

Explore dyslexia support →

S.P.A.R.C. & Focus Friday

Our proprietary STEAM enrichment, debate, visual arts, and Focus Friday experiences empower students to discover their authentic passions beyond traditional textbook boundaries.

Character & SRA Cares

Academic brilliance without empathy is hollow. Through monthly community service with Ronald McDonald House, local animal shelters, and literacy outreach, our students learn to lead with heart.
