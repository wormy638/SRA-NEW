Student Experience

Building Character, Friendship & Lifelong Memories

School is so much more than textbooks. At SRA, campus life is packed with sportsmanship, community service, nature expeditions, and joyful creativity.

Eagle Athletics

Home of the SRA Eagles

Physical movement and teamwork are vital components of brain health and character development.

Our athletic program emphasizes participation, skill progression, perseverance, and respectful competition. Students in Primary and Middle grades compete in basketball, volleyball, cross-country, and cheer, guided by encouraging coaches who reinforce sportsmanship above all else.

✓ Interscholastic league competition for Middle School grades
✓ Primary developmental clinics: basketball, soccer, and cross-country
✓ No-cut policy in developmental sports to encourage confidence
Community Outreach

SRA Cares: Leading with Compassion

We believe that leadership without empathy is meaningless. SRA Cares teaches our children that their voices and hands have the power to heal their community.

Every month, our students spearhead real community service projects: baking cookies for Ronald McDonald House families, collecting blankets for animal shelters, conducting book drives for under-resourced preschools, and writing letters to veterans.

✓ Monthly school-wide service learning drives and initiatives
✓ Direct partnerships with Ronald McDonald House and local charities
✓ Student-led philanthropy clubs and volunteer hour tracking
Student Support Services

Supporting Busy Families Every Step of the Day

Convenient, healthy, and enriching services that keep your child engaged and supported from morning until late afternoon.

Fresh Lunch Program

Nutritious, balanced lunches prepared fresh daily. Our menu provides hot meals, wholesome salads, and fresh fruits with simple online ordering for parents through the FACTS portal.

⏰

After-School Program (Stay & Play)

Available Monday through Friday until 5:30 PM. Students enjoy healthy snacks, supervised outdoor playtime, homework support, board games, and quiet reading in a safe, loving environment.

SRA Summer Camps

Keep the joy alive all summer! Our weekly summer camps feature outdoor science adventures, robotics, culinary arts, cheer, theater, and literacy acceleration for ages 3 through 12.
