State Financial Assistance

Making Exceptional Education Affordable

Savannah River Academy actively participates in all major State of Georgia tax-credit tuition assistance programs.

⭐

Georgia GOAL Scholarship

The Georgia GOAL Scholarship Program allows Georgia taxpayers to redirect their state tax dollars to provide private school tuition scholarships for qualified students transferring from public schools or entering Pre-K/Kindergarten.

Georgia Special Needs Scholarship (GSNS)

Students with an Individualized Education Plan (IEP), 504 Plan, or eligible diagnosis (including Dyslexia) may receive direct state scholarship vouchers toward SRA tuition and specialized dyslexia tutoring.

Need-Based Tuition Assistance

Through our private SRA Annual Fund and generous community donors, we provide supplemental need-based financial aid to ensure socioeconomic diversity in our school family.
