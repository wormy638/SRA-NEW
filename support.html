The Savannah River Academy Annual Fund

Like all premier independent schools, tuition alone does not cover the complete operating cost of maintaining an 8:1 student-to-teacher ratio, master faculty, specialized Orton-Gillingham reading specialists, and cutting-edge laboratory science.

Where Your Gift Makes an Immediate Impact

Annual Fund gifts are 100% tax-deductible and are put to work directly during the current academic year. Your contribution directly funds:

✓ Faculty Excellence & Certifications: Advanced training in the Science of Reading, Orton-Gillingham methodologies, and Singapore Math pedagogy.
✓ Classroom & STEAM Upgrades: Robotics kits, 3D printing equipment, science laboratory glassware, and multi-sensory math manipulatives.
✓ Need-Based Tuition Assistance: Enabling deserving students from diverse socioeconomic backgrounds to experience the life-changing gift of SRA.
✓ Nature Classrooms & Campus Safety: Enhancing outdoor learning pavilions, nature discovery trails, and security infrastructure.

Zero-Cost Tax Redirection

Georgia GOAL Scholarship Program for Taxpayers

Redirect Georgia state income taxes that you already owe directly into scholarships for Savannah River Academy students. You receive a 100% dollar-for-dollar tax credit on your Georgia state tax return!

Official State-Approved SSO Partner

Turn Your Georgia Tax Dollars Into SRA Tuition Aid

Important Note: Participation in the Georgia GOAL program is not an additional charitable expense—it is a dollar-for-dollar redirection of tax liability you already owe to the State of Georgia. Instead of sending those tax dollars to Atlanta, you tell Georgia to send them directly to Savannah River Academy!

In addition to receiving a 100% Georgia income tax credit, business owners and individuals who itemize can often claim federal charitable tax deductions as well.

Apply Online at GoalScholarship.org ↗

2026 Georgia Tax Credit Limits

Single Filer: Up to $2,500
Married Filing Jointly: Up to $5,000
Pass-Through Business Owners: Up to $25,000
C-Corporations & Trusts: Up to 75% of GA Tax Liability
* Applications open beginning June 1st and are submitted to the Georgia Department of Revenue (DOR) on the first business day of January.

How Simple Is It to Participate?

01

Apply Online in 3 Mins

Complete the short form at goalscholarship.org naming Savannah River Academy as your recipient school.

02

DOR Approval

GOAL submits to the Georgia Dept of Revenue in January. You are notified of your approved credit amount.

03

Remit Payment

Pay GOAL by check, credit card, or ACH within 60 days of approval (by mid-March).

04

Claim Your 100% Credit

GOAL sends your official Form IT-QEE-SSO1 tax receipt to claim the 100% credit on your Georgia state tax return.

Parent Service Organization (PSO)

At Savannah River Academy, we are not just a school—we are a tight-knit family of dedicated parents and educators working hand-in-hand to enrich our children’s lives.

Our Mission & Purpose

The PSO develops an authentic partnership between school leadership and families by fostering parent involvement, organizing campus-wide celebrations, executing teacher appreciation weeks, and directing targeted fundraisers that directly improve student life.

Every SRA family is automatically a member of the PSO. Whether you have one hour a month or one day a week, your time, passion, and unique talents are welcomed and celebrated.
