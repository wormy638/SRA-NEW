Academic Excellence

A Rigorous, Multi-Sensory Educational Journey

From our early childhood nature classrooms to laboratory science and specialized dyslexia intervention, we equip students with the tools to think critically, question fearlessly, and lead with empathy.

Early Childhood • Ages 3 – 4

The Nest: From Wonder to Wisdom

Cognitive research shows that 80% of human brain development takes place before the age of five.

The Nest honors this critical window through a rich, multi-sensory curriculum where children learn through play, story, sensory exploration, and daily outdoor investigation. Our outdoor classroom turns the natural world into a living laboratory where curiosity and wonder flourish.

✓ Daily outdoor nature classroom exploration and gross-motor development
✓ Multi-sensory phonemic awareness and emergent math numeracy
✓ Nurturing social-emotional skills, conflict resolution, and self-regulation
Grades K through 5th

Primary Academy: Unlocking Mastery at an 8:1 Ratio

No child learns at the exact same pace. Our 8:1 ratio ensures every student is truly seen, heard, and championed.

Our Language Arts curriculum is grounded in the Science of Reading, pairing research-based systematic phonics with rich literary comprehension. In mathematics, we utilize hands-on manipulatives and Singapore Math frameworks to build profound conceptual reasoning before procedural memorization.

✓ 8:1 student-to-teacher ratio in all core academic classrooms
✓ Structured literacy: decoding, morphology, vocabulary, and creative writing
✓ Integrated STEAM, physical movement, visual art, and music daily
Grades 6th through 8th

Middle School: Exploration, Agency & Leadership

Middle schoolers need high expectations coupled with genuine trust and adult mentorship.

Our Middle School curriculum transitions students from consumers of knowledge into creative producers. Through laboratory biology, algebra, persuasive rhetoric, and real-world expeditions, students develop the intellectual discipline and executive function required to thrive in top competitive high schools.

✓ Annual learning expeditions: Space Camp, coastal marine ecology, Washington D.C.
✓ High school prep coaching: study strategies, note-taking, and time management
✓ Interscholastic athletics, speech, debate, and Student Council leadership
Specialized Intervention

Dyslexia Tutoring Program: Built Into the School Day

Dyslexia affects 1 in 5 individuals. At SRA, dyslexia is never treated as a disorder—it is simply a different neurological wiring that thrives under explicit, multi-sensory instruction.

Instead of forcing tired children and exhausted parents into grueling 5:00 PM tutoring sessions across town, SRA embeds certified Orton-Gillingham 1-on-1 tutoring directly into your child’s school schedule.

✓ Daily 1-on-1 sessions with Certified Orton-Gillingham practitioners
✓ Explicit, systematic, tactile phonics, morphology, and syllable division
✓ Eligible for Georgia Special Needs Scholarship (GSNS) tax credit funding
Signature Enrichments

S.P.A.R.C. & Focus Friday

Specialized programs designed to ignite passion, cultivate creativity, and connect classroom concepts to real-world impact.

⚡

S.P.A.R.C. Enrichment

Science, Performance, Arts, Robotics & Creativity. Students step away from standard desks into design workshops where they build circuits, code robotics, sketch architectural models, and stage theatrical productions.

📅

Focus Friday

Fridays at SRA break the mold. Rather than traditional routine, students participate in deep-dive investigative projects, field trips to local nature reserves, visiting scientist seminars, and student-directed portfolio exhibitions.

🎭

Performing Arts & Music

Confidence is vocalized on stage. All students engage in public speaking, choral music, and drama to build presence, poise, and expressive articulation before diverse audiences.
