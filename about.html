Who We Are

Nurturing Inquisitive Minds & Compassionate Hearts

Savannah River Academy was founded on a simple yet revolutionary truth: children achieve their greatest academic potential when they feel safe, known, and excited to learn.

Our Philosophy

Rigor Defined by Depth, Not Stress.

At SRA, we reject the notion that academic rigor requires hours of repetitive homework, endless standardized test drills, and stressful memorization. True rigor is deep curiosity, scientific experimentation, conceptual math mastery, and analytical debate.

With our signature 8:1 student-to-teacher ratio, our teachers have the freedom and time to meet every student at their exact point of need. Advanced students are never held back, and students who need additional reinforcement receive targeted support immediately.

Our Mission & Core Beliefs

Every decision we make at Savannah River Academy is guided by four core commitments to our students and families.

🌱

Joyful Discovery

Children naturally yearn to explore their world. We protect and nurture that innate spark through experiential science, outdoor classrooms, and arts immersion.

🎯

Cognitive Diversity

Every brain is unique. We celebrate neurodiversity, specializing in Orton-Gillingham structured literacy for students with dyslexia and tailored acceleration for gifted learners.

❤️

Compassion & Empathy

Character is forged through action. Our students participate in regular service projects, learning that their intellect is meant to lift up their community.
